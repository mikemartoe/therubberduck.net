OSS

Office of Strategic Services

The thread 1942 OSS → 1945 shut down → 1947 CIA → 1961 Kennedy moves paramilitary work toward the military → 2016 Congress → 2020 Miller elevation → 2021 Austin rollback → 2026 Hegseth finishes it under the OSS name.

On November 2, 2026, made public November 12, Secretary of War Pete Hegseth elevates the Assistant Secretary of War for Special Operations and Low-Intensity Conflict, ASW(SO/LIC). He puts the office on equal footing with the military department secretaries and gives it the title "Assistant Secretary of War for the Office of Strategic Services," ASW(OSS).

Hegseth memorandum · OSD011630-26 It gets at least $45 million a year, 100 staff slots, and its own budget and buying authority, and he asks Congress for a permanent Under Secretary of Strategic Services. It does not recreate the wartime OSS or replace the CIA, and it isn't a new military branch.
