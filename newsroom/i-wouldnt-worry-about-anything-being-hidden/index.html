I wouldn’t worry about anything being hidden…

By The Rubber Duck™ · @TheRubberDuck79 · Original on X

I wouldn't worry about anything being hidden, at this point folks. I was butthurt and upset yesterday just like many of you because I'm a normal person not an npc.

truth is - there was no outrage in real life - only here and around the internet. the people who are not awake yet don't think like we do, therefore will not respond to redpilling in the traditional sense, and certainly not from us - we've tried for 8 years.

----------

what's funny is that this is the most important group that must now reach that critical point of consciousness all together and on their own without Q drops or redpills, we won't be able to help them with what they see until the other side, if that makes sense to you - and we will be there for that. but it's not now I don't think.

it's clear that they are setting the stage, gauging the algorithms and media engagement for what the remaining sleeping public knows and at what rate it can consume this disclosure op.

a more impatient anon does not exist than me - and after sleeping on it - I thoroughly believe patriots are in control and are laying disclosure crumbs while setting the stage for a precipice or false flag TV event to bridge the gap between the awake and the sleeping.

hence; learn the plan, trust the plan and enjoy the show because we are watching a movie & nothing can stop what is coming.
