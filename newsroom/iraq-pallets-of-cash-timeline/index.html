IRAQ Pallets of Cash: From Bush → Obama → Biden → A Timeline of U.S. Cash Movements Through Iraq

By The Rubber Duck™ · @TheRubberDuck79 · Original on X

Share this dispatch Front page · platforms below

Between 2003 and 2016, the United States oversaw the transfer of tens of billions of dollars in cash into Iraq — the largest physical movement of currency in modern history.

👉 A significant portion of that money cannot be fully accounted for today.

👉 And despite years of congressional hearings, audits, and investigations:

👉 no single authority has ever produced a complete accounting of where it all went.

2003–2004 — The Airlift Begins (Bush Administration)

  • After the U.S. invasion of Iraq, the Coalition Provisional Authority (CPA) governed Iraq from April 2003 to June 2004.
  • The Federal Reserve shipped massive amounts of physical U.S. currency to Baghdad on military aircraft.
  • Total shipments reached hundreds of tons of cash on pallets.

👉 Key facts:

  • 363 tons of cash
  • 484 pallets
  • 281 million individual banknotes
  • ~$12 billion flown in during the first phase

👉 Source of funds:

  • Iraqi oil revenue
  • Surplus UN Oil-for-Food program money
  • Frozen assets of Saddam Hussein’s regime

👉 Example shipments:

  • $1.5B – Dec 12, 2003
  • $2.4B – June 22, 2004
  • $1.6B – June 25, 2004

These shipments occurred days before the U.S. handed sovereignty back to Iraq.

👉 Congressional reaction:

  • Rep. Henry Waxman: “Who in their right mind would send 363 tons of cash into a war zone?”

2005 — Billions Already Missing

👉 Special Inspector General Stuart Bowen reported:

  • $8.8 billion unaccounted for after being distributed to Iraqi ministries.

👉Auditors discovered:

  • Weak internal controls
  • Failure to follow basic financial rules
  • Security lapses around cash storage

👉 Result:

  • Billions of dollars could not be traced.

2003–2008 — The Total Grows to ~$40 Billion

👉 Investigations later revealed:

  • The New York Federal Reserve ultimately shipped about $40 billion in cash to Iraq.
  • The operation became the largest airborne currency transfer in history.

👉Transport chain:

  1. Cash packed at the New York Fed facility
  2. Trucked to Andrews Air Force Base
  3. Loaded on C-17 military cargo planes
  4. Flown directly to Baghdad

👉 At times:

  • One CPA official was responsible for receiving billions of dollars per flight.

👉 Chain-of-custody:

  • Clear in the U.S.
  • Unclear once the money entered Iraq.

2010–2014 — Investigators Track Stolen Cash Overseas

👉 Bowen’s investigation later uncovered:

  • $1.2B – $1.6B stolen
  • Hidden in a bunker in rural Lebanon

👉 But:

  • The case stalled.
  • U.S. agencies declined deeper pursuit.
  • The investigation closed without full recovery.

Bowen:

“Billions of dollars have been taken out of Iraq illegally.”

2012 — Biden Family Appears in Iraq Contracting

👉 Separate reporting during the Obama administration revealed:

  • James Biden (Joe Biden’s brother) involved in a company tied to a $1.5B housing contract in Iraq.

👉 Context:

  • The company’s leadership cited Biden family connections as an advantage in securing work.

2016 — Another Cash Transfer Controversy (Iran)

👉 Obama administration payment:

  • $400M delivered in physical foreign cash
  • $1.3B additional settlement payments

👉 Reason given:

  • Resolution of a decades-old arms dispute.

👉 What it really was:

  • The payment coincided with release of American prisoners and resembled a ransom transaction.

Questions That Still Exist:

  1. Why were tens of billions moved as physical cash instead of controlled banking transfers?
  2. Why were basic accounting controls missing during the CPA period?
  3. Why were billions never recovered despite years of investigation?
  4. Why did multiple administrations decline deeper investigation?
