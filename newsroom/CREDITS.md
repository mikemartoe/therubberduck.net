# DUCK SAUCE — credits

## Background photos

The DUCK SAUCE collage (`images/newsroom/collage-desktop.webp`, `collage-mobile.webp`) is made from Mike’s six theme photos of soldiers with *Stars and Stripes* / field newspapers (Vietnam through Iraq / Kosovo / desert deployment). Source files live at `images/newsroom/ducksauce-src/`. Cropped and scaled only for the collage; not otherwise altered.

| File | Subject |
|---|---|
| 01-desert-deployment.png | Desert deployment — soldier reading paper |
| 02-iraq-baghdad.png | Iraq troops by truck tire — “GIs nearing Baghdad” |
| 03-kosovo-4000.png | Kosovo soldier, red glasses — “4,000 to Kosovo” |
| 04-vietnam-home-safe.png | Vietnam GIs — “HOME SAFE” / “HOME SAFELY” |
| 05-gi-capture-major-base.png | Vietnam — “GI’S CAPTURE ‘MAJOR BASE’” |
| 06-accuse-portrait.png | Shirtless soldier in sunglasses — “ACCUSE” |

Older public-domain newsroom-desk photos under `images/newsroom/pd/` are leftover from the Nov 1 preview and are **not** used in the DUCK SAUCE collage.
