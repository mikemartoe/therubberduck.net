HOW TO leave the 9 - 5 slave system…

By The Rubber Duck™ · @TheRubberDuck79 · Original on X

HOW TO leave the 9 - 5 slave system without skipping a beat or disrupting your life in any way shape or form.

👉🏼 I will roll this out over a series of posts, or perhaps I'll make a thread. MUST BE one step at a time because each step requires mastery - and mastering any new behavior takes a minimum of two weeks committed repetition, or no bueno. if you do not believe in God and/or Jesus Christ - keep scrolling, this is not for you and will not work...

i'll walk you through this and answer any questions. maybe we'll do some spaces and talk about it. I will not leave you behind. i've lived this for 35 years, taught this and mentored this publicly in seminar and classroom format across the country to students, directors, managers, leaders as well as sales departments and midsize companies seeking to grow in ways that serve, not deplete - something difficult and not typical in the corporate environment.

👉🏼 PART 1

the process starts spiritually [in your mind] where you are now by putting God first while "at work".

keep doing what you're doing - but learn to maintain your spiritual connection to God at all times.

👎🏼 EXCUSES: "I can't do this while I'm working, I need to stay focused on work." or "I'm the president or director of a corporation - I can't be seen "meditating" at work" or "I can't, I'm embarrassed."

✅ I do this by consciously stopping whatever I'm doing every half hour or an hour and saying aloud to myself [and God], "thank you God, thank you Jesus, bless you God, bless you Jesus, I trust you God I trust you Jesus." now that's just me - this is how I maintain my spiritual connection, which keeps me from straying too far from my spiritual track and into the idle mind where satan lives and waits. you can make up & do whatever you want to achieve this, but you must speak aloud to God [not yourself] - and not to ask - but to acknowledge, give thanks and your trust, no more/no less than that.

** you must speak the words out loud to make it manifest. the words, sounds and vibrations of your voice carry as much power and spirit as the spells they cast upon us. trust me - it feels awkward and weird initially, but soon with repetition you find yourself walking around in the spirit no matter what you do. you'll soon find yourself spiritually prepared for the slings and arrows that attack your spirit every second of every day without your knowledge or awareness.

🎯 God never walks away from us, He is always there in the same place, He is the constant consistent. it is we, with the free will we are blessed with that strays away from Him, and that's when we fall vulnerable to satan and his evil thoughts and ideas that take us further and further away from our spirit, God and truth - and eventually off track.

this causes stress, anxiety, fear, panic, sadness, loneliness and depression while in a work environment - which equals bad days, moodiness, grouchy and consistent "ruts".

👉🏼👉🏼👉🏼 start here. have fun with it. practice it constantly. find what works for you. soon you'll bring this into your life outside of the 9 - 5 weekday, but that will come later.

... let me know how you all make out in the comments. consistency is what's most important. when you go to bed at night while doing this, you should be able to tell yourself you stayed with God the whole day and not lie.

👉🏼 this is not a cure for anything, this is how to live your life alongside the system without becoming a part of the system - at least until this system is destroyed and gone forever.
