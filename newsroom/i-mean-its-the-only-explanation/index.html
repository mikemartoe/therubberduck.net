I mean it’s the only explanation…

By The Rubber Duck™ · @TheRubberDuck79 · Original on X

Share this dispatch Front page · platforms below

I mean it's the only explanation - once the masses find out what they did and what they've been doing all along - and for how long.

here's a story I made up.

follow me here...

humans don't behave that way towards other humans - particularly; the unborn, newborns, toddlers and children.

humans don't treat their pets that way. humans don't even treat their enemies or food that way.

BUT - there is a very small group spread across earth that do behave like this - especially to their food and towards their enemies - which I believe to be humanity and God, at large; we who behave civilly towards each other with dignity and respect - particularly those who believe in God, Jesus Christ, Family, Country and those who simply yearn to live in love.

I also don't believe they come from above or outer space - mostly because that's where they've been telling us to look all along. like it or not there is a firmament and we see Elon bouncing shit off of it and alongside it all the time - so just stop.

I think they come from beneath the surface and have their own abilities - including looking like us, but not the ability to behave, believe or create like us - because they're not human, only in form. but it seems through their technology [satan] - they became able to "plug into" and essentially hack humanity through our "aura" - giving them access to our essence, our spirit and what they perceived to be our soul.

and maybe that was God's "last straw" to satan.

I think this has been the fight all along that has come to the surface over the last 10 years - for whatever the reason.

I believe a war for humanity was fought and won - in what will only be able to be explained as - by "God's Hand in Grace" and the US Military.

they're gonna show us whatever they want on every single flat black screen on earth and they'll probably find a way to fake it in our reality if they need to, because God's victors will get to write this history and everyone will have a choice between good and evil to make - and it will be that clear.

*NOTE: I could be wrong.
