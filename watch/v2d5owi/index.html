The Late William Bill Cooper - Global Deception Conference GDC, Wembley Arena Conference Centre, London UK - January 9-10, 1993.
What Bill Cooper talks about is highly interesting - as you'd have expected from Bill. His lecture is almost three hours long. Bill talks about the history of the mystery schools and its hidden secrets, and how the Illuminati want to take over the world and to what degree they've already done so. This looks like Bill's final warning to the British people. Then Bill outlines the symbols on the one-Dollar bill in much detail. Bill also discusses the famous Zapruder film showing the final moments of JFK's life.
This is the lecture Bill gave to 'Global Deception Conference' held in Wembley Arena Conference Centre, London in January of 1993 (the 9th through the 10th) about the New World Order and their plans for the globe. He goes over some of their employed methods, how their ends justifies the means, and how their religion doesn't have to be believed by anyone else for it to still have effect over the entire populous. This is one of the most amazing lectures you'll hear during your lifetime, guaranteed.
