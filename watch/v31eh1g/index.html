Milton William "Bill" Cooper - The Lost Interview, 1999

July 21, 2023 · Length 10:47 · 5.77K views on Rumble

many people do not know that Milton William "Bill" Cooper changed his views on UFO's 8 years after writing Behold A Pale Horse.
Cooper discovered a document that influenced his change of heart. it was the "Carnegie Endowment For International Peace", written in 1917. here is the link to the document: https://bit.ly/3rEpd9O
page 105 of this document has the text of a speech given by John Dewey, Professor of Philosophy in Columbia University at that time. He said:
"... the best way to unite all the nations on this globe would be an attack from some other planet. In the face of such an alien enemy, people would respond with a sense of their unity of interest and purpose."
that was 1917.
