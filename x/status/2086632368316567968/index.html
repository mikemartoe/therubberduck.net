if you've not already - buying as much locally grown & raised fresh food… | The X Archive | The Rubber Duck™

if you've not already - buying as much locally grown & raised fresh food should be your goal with everything you eat and drink, if you don't have the means or wherewithal to grow and raise food yourself.

learn where the fresh food you eat comes from - just ask, or check the label for where it's sourced. open your AI when you shop, and take pictures of the stickers and labels on your fresh food and it will tell you all you need to know - including what it's sprayed with.

feed your entire month's grocery list into your AI and prompt it to source everything on your list locally. get as close as you can. it's a fun and healthy excercise.
