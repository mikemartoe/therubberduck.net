FACTS

The Military Reports To The Executive Branch. The President, as Commander-in-Chief, has ultimate authority over the armed forces and the Department of Defense with the Secretary of Defense (Pete Hegseth) overseeing military operations and the Joint Chiefs of Staff (Dan 'Raizin' Caine).

There Are 3 Branches Of Government:

1.) Legislative Branch: This branch is responsible for creating laws and is composed of Congress, which includes the House of Representatives and the Senate.

2.) Judicial Branch: This branch interprets laws and includes the Supreme Court and other federal courts.

3.) Executive Branch: This branch enforces the laws passed by Congress and is led by the President, who also has the power to veto laws.
