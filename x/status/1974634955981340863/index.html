‼️THE INSURRECTION ACT‼️ is codified principally in 10 U.S.C. §§ 331–335. Defense Policy

The main operative sections are:

• Section 331: The President may call forth the militia (or use the regular armed forces) upon request from a State’s legislature or its governor (if the legislature can’t convene), when there is an insurrection against the state government.

• Section 332: The President may act without state consent in situations where “unlawful obstructions, combinations, or assemblages, or rebellion” make it “impracticable” to enforce federal law by ordinary means.

• Section 333: The President may intervene when “any insurrection, domestic violence, unlawful combination, or conspiracy” so hinders the execution of federal law that rights are deprived or the state is unable or unwilling to protect them.

These provisions allow the President to:

• Federalize the National Guard (or “militia”) of any state (including ones not requesting help).

• Use regular U.S. armed forces.

• Temporarily override the Posse Comitatus restrictions for law enforcement - in those type actions in those specific circumstances.

**Posse Comitatus prevents federal troops from being used as a domestic police.
