47 U.S.C. § 606 is a section of the United States Code that deals with the War Powers of the President. It provides the President with the authority to suspend or amend, for a limited time, the rules and regulations applicable to certain communication stations or devices in the interest of national security or defense during a state of war or other national emergency.

Donald Trump declared a national emergency on March 13, 2020, in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. The declaration was made under the National Emergencies Act, which allows the president to declare a national emergency in response to a threat to the nation. This declaration gave the federal government additional powers to respond to the pandemic, including the ability to waive certain regulations and allocate additional funds for the response.

That declaration by Donald Trump on March 13, 2020, is still in effect today.
