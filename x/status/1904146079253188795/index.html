The USS Kidd (DDG-100), an Arleigh Burke-class guided-missile destroyer, is the only U.S. Navy surface ship officially authorized to fly the Jolly Roger flag. This unique privilege stems from a tradition that began with the first USS Kidd (DD-661), a Fletcher-class destroyer commissioned in 1943. Named after Rear Admiral Isaac C. Kidd, who died during the Pearl Harbor attack in 1941, the ship’s crew adopted a pirate theme due to the admiral’s nickname “Cap,” which echoed the infamous pirate Captain William Kidd. With the support of Kidd’s widow, Inez, the Navy granted formal permission for the ship to fly the Jolly Roger and paint a pirate image on its smokestack. This tradition has carried over to subsequent ships bearing the Kidd name, including the current USS Kidd (DDG-100), commissioned in 2007.
