here's a rant...

not the least bit interested in eating any type of meat, fish, poultry or eggs unless I can meet the people killing the animals and collecting the eggs - or get as high up that chain as I can where I can look someone in the eye.

I would never kill an animal myself unless I had to in order to eat or feed my family and something just doesn't sit right with me all of these animals being raised purely for slaughter - it just doesn't seem right, or necessary. I could be wrong, and I really don't care - it's just where I'm at.

and I don't want anything "blessed" by a rabbi - i'll pray and give thanks to MY GOD over my own food, thank you. pfft

same thing goes for produce - I don't want to have to sift through little stickers and labels on my fruits and vegetables in a supermarket and then match them up against a chart on my cell phone to decide which poison is the least lethal that I KNOW has been sprayed on everything that I should buy and serve to my family.

my goal, and I'm doing this with my family - is to not consume any ingredient as food that I can't source & trust myself.

they told us that the foods in supermarkets were "processed" in order so that they could last longer, giving more people the ability to have access to it over time - that's what they told me when I was a kid.

anyway, I call bullshit on the whole thing - I'm out.
