in the absence of a “Grand Authenticator” - who is to decide what is true or… | The X Archive | The Rubber Duck™

in the absence of a “Grand Authenticator” - who is to decide what is true or real ever again; outside of what you can physically see, hear, touch, taste and smell?

once people realize that they cannot confirm a damn thing via the media - they will simply stop caring about what’s outside of physical circle of influence.

IMHO - only then will people find the power and the courage to turn the TV off and sign out of their phones and computers - and live their lives again.

screw all the bullshit convenience technology was supposed to bring, I don’t need to know everything everywhere every second of the day.

that’s what God is for.
