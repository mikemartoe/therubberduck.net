now I'm a little past the halfway mark of my apparent psyop life - here's a list of what I have determined, through experience alone, that I never needed to be a healthy happily married man of 30 years with 2 amazing well-adjusted sons who still live with me.

a big house with a picket fence.

a new car - or more than one.

the stock market or crypto.

a college education, or the need to send my kids far away to college to "spread their wings".

a boss, a career or a 9 to 5 job.

more than $5,000 in the bank at any given time.

health insurance. check-ups. vaccines.

alcohol, drugs, all the premarital sex.

a salary of more than $75k.

more than one or two friends outside my family.

church or memorization of the bible.

music, hollywood, TV, internet.

mobile devices.

credit cards.

a gym.

sports.

government assistance.

I have had & willfully enjoyed most everything on this list throughout my life, and a lot of it was fun.

at some points of my life, however rare, some of these things seemed important to "get over a hump".

I have also lost or had taken away from me, most on this list throughout my life. i've also willfully walked away from a lot on this list - and others I just never got into.

other than food, water, shelter, transportation and enough shekels to traverse the beast system without getting caught up in it is all I ever really needed and need from here on out to live out the rest of my days as a happy man.

the only two things I could not and would not be able to live without are my family, my relationship with God, and the means to protect them both.

fuck everything else.
