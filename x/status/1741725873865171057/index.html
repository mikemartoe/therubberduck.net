Jordan Maxwell "Symbols, Sex, and the Stars"

———————

#JordanMaxwell's seminar on "Symbols, Sex, and the Stars" delves into the origins and meanings of various symbols found in ancient civilizations. Maxwell argues that many symbols, such as the #pentagram and the #swastika, have been misunderstood and misinterpreted throughout history. He also discusses the use of symbols in religion, politics, and media.

One of the key themes in Maxwell's seminar is the connection between symbols and sex. He explores the use of phallic and yonic symbols in ancient cultures, as well as the role of sexuality in modern media and advertising.

Maxwell also examines the relationship between symbols and #astrology. He discusses the zodiac and its association with different symbols, as well as the use of astrology in ancient cultures and modern times.

Overall, Maxwell's seminar offers a fascinating look at the power and significance of symbols in human history and culture. It encourages viewers to question the meanings behind common symbols and to explore their own beliefs and values.
