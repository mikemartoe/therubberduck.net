Rustic Italian Bread with Sourdough Starter (Hybrid)

Ingredients:

•3 ½ cups (450g) bread flour or
“Tipo 00” flour
•¾ cup (180g) active sourdough
starter (fed and bubbly)
•¾ cup (180ml) warm water
•1 tsp (5g) salt
•1 tsp (4g) sugar or honey
•1 tsp (3g) active dry or instant
yeast
•1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil

Instructions

1.Activate Yeast (if dry): In a small bowl, dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water. Let sit 5–10 minutes until foamy.

2.Mix Dough: In a large bowl, combine the starter, yeast water, olive oil, and flour. Add salt last. Mix into a shaggy dough.

3.Knead: Knead on a floured surface for 8–10 minutes (or in a mixer 5–6 min) until smooth and elastic.

4.First Rise (Shortened): Cover and let rise in a warm place for 1 to 1.5 hours, or until nearly doubled.

5.Shape Loaf: Deflate gently, shape into a round or oval, and place on parchment or a baking sheet.

http://6.Final Rise: Let rest 30–45 minutes covered, until puffy but not overproofed.

7.Preheat & Bake: Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C). Add steam (pan of water or spritz). Slash the loaf and bake 25–30 minutes.

http://8.Cool: Rest at least 20–30 minutes to allow crumb to finish setting inside.
