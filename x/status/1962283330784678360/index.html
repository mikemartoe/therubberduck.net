"from 9/11 planes to elon's rockets."

facts to ponder:

Cubic Projectors or modular cube systems project images from multiple angles, effectively creating a 3D or hologram-like display, where light is directed onto particles, fog, or layered media inside a cubic enclosure, making an image that seems to float in space.

Optical Vertical Projection broadcasts holographic displays that se multi-axis vertical projection onto or into special media (like mist, nano-particle glass, or liquid crystal panels), where light is projected vertically and intersected by other beams, creating volumetric 3D images.

Plasma Holograms are created by ultra-focused lasers that ionize air molecules at a specific point, creating a visible glowing dot of plasma. then, by moving the laser rapidly, the system “draws” objects floating in midair and emit plasma pulses that can be felt & measured.
