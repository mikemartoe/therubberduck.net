#GavinNewsom succeeded #JerryBrown, his quasi-uncle, continuing the decades-long saga of four San Francisco families intertwined by blood, by marriage, by money, by culture and by politics – the Browns, the Newsoms, the Pelosis and the Gettys.

the connections date back at least 80 years, to when Jerry Brown’s father, Pat Brown, ran for San Francisco district attorney, losing in 1939 but winning in 1943, with the help of his close friend and Gavin Newsom’s grandfather, businessman William Newsom.

20 years later - Gov. Pat Brown’s administration developed Squaw Valley for the 1960s winter Olympics and afterward awarded a concession to operate it to William Newsom and his partner, John Pelosi.

one of the Pelosis’ sons, Paul, married Nancy D’Alesandro [#NancyPelosi]. another Pelosi son married William Newsom’s daughter, Barbara - making Nancy Pelosi Gavin Newsom’s aunt by marriage. Nancy Pelosi’s brother-in-law was Gavin Newsom’s uncle.
