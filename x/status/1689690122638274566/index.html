we ARE a Constitutional Republic.
we are NOT a democracy.
we are NO LONGER a corporation.
our debts are PAID.

if you are still alive - then you ARE NOT of the 4%-6% who did not make it to this point.

you were CHOSEN for these times now, and ahead - for good or bad. some will ascend to a higher level of consciousness - whatever that will look and feel like, while others will not.

there will always be evil in this world because we’re flawed earthly beings given to temptation. I strongly believe that this whole awakening process is about the internal balance of that evil [physical & spiritual] and about choice; personal choice. the choice to follow God, the choice to know the truth about our history - the children, the choice to believe your eyes and the choice to act on that knowledge in someway good, serving God and humanity.

only the godless made bad choices that cost them their lives. good people who have lived their lives doubting or hedging on whether God is real or those who submitted long ago to living a life of fear were probably vaxxed/boosted, so they are going to have worries that none of us will - like knowing you’ve played a game of russian roulette with a 1-3 year delay.

the ones that survive that worry will learn a valuable lesson that they will never forget that will be passed down for generations - about the importance of God in their lives, and what it means to be Godly. they will have learned that there is no “fence” to live on.

we were told GOD WINS, not us. WE that survive are the ancillary benefit.

if you were The Father, how else would you “teach the world a lesson” of a commensurate magnitude that would prevent the evil atrocities we are learning about now, from ever happening again - or at least for another thousand years?

I think this would work.
