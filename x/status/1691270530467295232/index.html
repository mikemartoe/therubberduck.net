it’s a show.
it’s not for us.
we know how this ends.
you know how this ends.
nothing can stop what’s coming.
nothing.

military is the only way.

every other￼ option that you could possibly think of and list will be crossed off one by one by one.

until you trust the plan.

don’t think for a moment that this is a volunteer situation. no one will be able to escape or sit this one out. there are no sidelines - all the world is in play.

there is only one plan and one way out of this - we know this because it’s already happened.￼ past proves future and vice versa.

I’ve already faced that point in my awakening where it seemed as though everything I was learning was leading me through a funnel that would spit me out in front of just 2 choices - for all the marbles, where I was literally forced to chose one, instantly, in order to sanely continue a life of any value or meaning.

it was in that moment I realized - every second of life represents a choice to live that second on God’s path for good, or satan’s evil lonely path. I really believe it’s that simple. one can’t live without the other, but it’s up to us to keep the balance on the good side - willfully and knowingly.

you will learn that everything comes down to choice, and in the end, when there simply are no other choices or moves to make - only 2 will present themselves in a very obvious way; good or evil.

that’s the only way I was able to “put aside” 45 years of lies burned into my memory to allow for this new knowledge and understanding of how things really are, albeit horrific. but I know there’s a plan, and I understand it more every day.

if “they” come for me or my family - which means I’m wrong here - I will die on this hill believing in this plan, not a tv spell cast by satan.

consider that the moments in your life when you are at your worst, and ponder whether at that worst moment - was it you that stepped away from God, or the other way around.

food for thought. I pray it makes good sense. 🙏🏻

DON’T FALL INTO THE TV/CERN SPELL.

this goes for me & you. 👊🏼
