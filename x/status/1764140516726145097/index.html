‼️PSA: NO PLANE HIT THE SECOND TOWER ON 9/11‼️

ask any graphic designer, digital video editor or special effects tech - and every one of them will tell you that it would be nearly impossible to remove a plane from these clips.

HOWEVER

adding one via layering/masking = 17 minutes or less [in 2001].

which is exactly how much time they had from when flight 11 crashed into the North Tower [8:46 a.m.] and 9:03 a.m. - when "flight 175 crashed into the South Tower", allegedly.

there was no live feed of tower 2 exploding "from the plane crash" either. that was a live illusion.

go back and look at the live feed from that day - the live feeds were fixed on Tower 1 because that's where the alleged first plane crash was.

it is within that frame [ ] that only the top of the fireball from the explosion of Tower 2 could be seen. in fact, you can see the camera fixed on the live stream immediately pan down a little bit and left a little bit to the exploding fireball. no plane.

you'll appreciate this when you find it yourself.

... so they had 17 minutes in between both explosions - together with a delayed livestream - providing more than enough time, distraction and cover for an average graphic designer with nerves of steel to digitally layer a plane onto a few second clip of footage.

they did a shoddy job as a result; as the nose of the plane slid over the edge of the "mask" - which made it look like the nose popped through the other side of the south tower - a physical impossibility.

also there is a popular "plane crash" ground-level view from the east looking up at the west side of Tower 2 where the same thing happened...

they created masks of all the buildings in front of, adjacent to, and behind the towers [copies, which were layered atop each other, leaving an empty layer in between] - so they would have the depth necessary to add the digital plane into that empty middle layer and make it look like it was swooping in low enough to "bank" into the tower - BUT without masking - you would have seen the left wing of the plane hitting the buildings before the tower without "really hitting them [remember the perspective of this shot is looking up - there were plenty of other buildings around that had to be accounted for].

well, they got every building masked except one - a church. so now when you see that clip again, you will actually see the left wing of the plane literally disappear "behind" the church, as it flies by.

this could've only happened if they were layering & masking. it was a common mistake at the time. still is for me as I still use old software. takes a few minutes to fix, but they didn't have that - it was the worth the risk, because it was part of their plan and everyone was in on it.

that's been my story - and explanation for what I did not see that day - and I'm sticking to it.
