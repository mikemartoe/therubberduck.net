⁉️LEFTOVER SOURDOUGH STARTER⁉️

Sourdough Discard Flatbread (Pizza Stone or Skillet Method)

⸻

Ingredients
•1 cup sourdough discard
•1 cup all-purpose flour
•1 Tbsp olive oil
•½ tsp salt

(Optional: pinch of sugar for extra browning)

Step-by-Step

1️⃣ Preheat HARD
•Put pizza stone or cast-iron skillet on middle rack
•Oven to 500°F (or max your oven allows)
•Preheat at least 45 minutes

👉 This is the secret to bubbles.

⸻

2️⃣ Make the Dough
•Mix all ingredients until shaggy
•Knead lightly (30–60 sec)
•Rest 10 minutes (important for stretch)

Dough should be soft, not sticky. Add flour only if needed.

⸻

3️⃣ Shape
•Divide into 2–4 balls
•Roll or stretch very thin (⅛” or less)
•Dust with flour or semolina

Think naan meets pita thickness.

⸻

4️⃣ Bake
•Slide onto hot stone (parchment OK for first few tries)
•Bake 2–4 minutes
•Flip once if needed
•Pull when puffed, blistered, lightly charred

⸻

🔥 Optional Finish (Highly Recommended)

Brush with:
•Olive oil + salt
•Garlic butter
•Chili oil + honey
