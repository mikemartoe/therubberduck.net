I learned this at a young age at a #JackCanfield seminar.

when you apply this equation to your life, and then commit your life to living according to this equation, it completely eliminates any of the power associated with fear.

for me, the hardest part at first was to understand, and then own - was that I literally have nothing to do with the letter E in this equation; nada, zip. no matter what, we can’t change, control, impact or influence the events that occur upon us during our lives.

but once you really truly understand that, for good or bad, events just happen - it makes the R in this equation that much more powerful - in fact, the most powerful part of the equation because once you realize and make a conscious decision [in real time] about how to respond to a given event - you realize just how powerful you can be.

restraint is just as powerful as rage, if it’s the commensurate response to an event - and you choose right.

when you learn how to control the R in real time, there is no fear associated with the outcome because you took care of that by responding accordingly to an event. the outcome is your reality; your present mental and emotional state.

I hope this makes sense. 👊🏼
