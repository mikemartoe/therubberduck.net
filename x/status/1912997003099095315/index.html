‼️DID YOU KNOW‼️ in January, 2021 - just minutes before President Donald Trump left office, a Pentagon program that controlled 6% of the entire internet [175 million IP addresses] was routed to a company named Global Resource Systems in Plantation, Florida. this section of the internet is called IPv4.

the program was designed to detect unspecified "vulnerabilities" and prevent unauthorized use of DoD IP address space.

Trump transferred "control" of these IP addresses to the "Biden Administration" at 11:57 a.m. on Inauguration Day, three minutes before President Joe Biden took office - or did he?

the internet traffic of ALL 175 million IP addresses STILL flows to a computer router in Ashburn, Va., a major hub of internet connections for government agencies and private companies.

weird.
