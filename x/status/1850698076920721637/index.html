HOW DID THEY DO IT?

all you need to know.😎

-------------

‼️The Alien Enemies Act of 1798 [originally Alien and Sedition Act] can be invoked by a President under the following conditions‼️:

1. **Declaration of War**: It can be enacted when the United States is in a declared war.

2. **Invasion or Predatory Incursion**: The act can also be activated during times of "invasion or predatory incursion" by a foreign nation or government.

--------------

🛑 WHAT AUTHORITY DOES THIS GIVE THE COMMANDER-IN-CHIEF?

The act allows the president to apprehend, restrain, secure, and remove all subjects of the hostile nation who are within the United States and to provide for the safekeeping of their property.

--------------

🛑 HOW IS IT ENACTED?

- The President must issue a public proclamation of the event, naming the hostile nation or government.
- This proclamation would set guidelines for the actions to be taken against alien enemies, which could include: arrest, detainment, or deportation of individuals from the hostile nation.

--------------

🛑 BUT HOW DID TRUMP DECLARE WAR WITHOUT DECLARING WAR?

ANSWER:

•War Powers Resolution
•Executive Orders
•National Emergencies Act
•Federal Laws & Orders
•Law Of War Manual
•Alien Enemies Act of 1798

--------------

🛑 BUT CONGRESS MUST APPROVE ANY DECLARATION OF WAR!!

❌ WRONG: The Constitution gives Congress the power to declare war, but the President is Commander in Chief of the Armed Forces. This duality has led to numerous instances where presidents have expanded military actions without a formal declaration of war, citing their role as Commander in Chief or the necessity for quick action.

--------------

🛑 BUT THIS HAS NEVER HAPPENED BEFORE TRUMP IS A DICTATOR!

❌ WRONG: HERE ARE 11 TIMES WHEN PRESIDENTS WENT TO WAR WITHOUT EITHER DECLARING IT - OR SEEKING CONGRESSIONAL APPROVAL - OR EITHER. ⬇️

- **Harry Truman**, 1950 - Korean War, citing the need for immediate action due to the invasion of South Korea by North Korea.

- **Dwight D. Eisenhower**, 1958 - Lebanon crisis, to support the pro-Western Lebanese government against internal rebellion.

- **John F. Kennedy**, 1961 - Bay of Pigs Invasion, a covert operation to overthrow Fidel Castro.

- **Lyndon B. Johnson**, 1964 - Vietnam War escalation after the Gulf of Tonkin incident, claiming attacks on U.S. ships necessitated response.

- **Richard Nixon**, 1970 - Cambodian Incursion, as part of the Vietnam War to attack Viet Cong bases.

- **Ronald Reagan**, 1983 - Invasion of Grenada, to protect U.S. nationals and restore order after a coup.

- **George H.W. Bush**, 1989 - Panama invasion (Operation Just Cause), to apprehend Manuel Noriega and combat drug trafficking.

- **Bill Clinton**, 1999 - Kosovo War, NATO action for humanitarian intervention without UN Security Council authorization.

- **George W. Bush**, 2001 - Afghanistan War, immediate response to the 9/11 attacks.

- **Barack Obama**, 2011 - Libya intervention, under NATO to enforce UN Security Council Resolution 1973 for civilian protection.

- **Donald Trump**, 2020 - Strike against Qasem Soleimani, citing imminent threats to U.S. personnel and interests.

--------------

🛑 BUT WHAT ABOUT THE NATO & UNITED NATIONS SOLDIERS ON OUR SOIL - WE ARE DOOMED!!

❌ WRONG: sometimes actions are taken under the auspices of NATO or the UN, where the U.S. leads the fight but does not need to declare war if the action is framed as part of a coalition effort or peacekeeping mission.
