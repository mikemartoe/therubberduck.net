In the 1973 film Jesus Christ Superstar, "King Herod's Song" is a pivotal scene where King Herod Antipas, portrayed as a flamboyant and mocking figure, confronts Jesus Christ. The scene is set after Jesus is arrested and brought before Herod, who is depicted as a decadent, self-indulgent ruler eager to see Jesus perform miracles for his entertainment.

The scene underscores the contrast between Herod's superficiality and Jesus' silent dignity, as Jesus refuses to engage with Herod's provocations, leading Herod to send him back to Pontius Pilate, where he was then crucified.
