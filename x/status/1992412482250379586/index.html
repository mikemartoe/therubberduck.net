I REMEMBER - October 20, 2001 - 39 days after 9/11 - Paul McCartney put together The Concert For New York that was held at Madison Square Garden, New York City. All the A-List stars, celebs and musicians there to help us "cope".

39 days after I saw the side of Tower 2 explode without a plane hitting it. 39 days after I got home that day and realized everyone in my life and the world had seen a plane on tv that really wasn't there and there was nothing I could do or say about it. truly maddening.

it was this night, while watching this concert - that it hit me - they were ALL in on it. I never said a word, I couldn't. people like me don't stop and we usually wreck shit when we're right, but not this time. I caved like a cheap suit; let them put me on PTSD meds, and anything else that would numb my reality. I became a "bottle-a-night" alcoholic and a world class binge-eater - willfully gaining 230 pounds.

THEN this song came on, "Won't Get Fooled Again" by The Who - one of my all-time favorites - and I just remember crying so hard and for so long and promising I WON"T GET FOOLED AGAIN! with my drunken courage. I remember running out of tears, but still crying, and no sound coming out, but still crying. I stopped dreaming shortly after that, some meds [+++] will do that. when I lay down to sleep, I wake up with no memory of falling asleep or dreaming - to this day.

looking back now - I realize we really were fooled again - and that concert was a mock. one of many.

I did not intend for this to be a depressing post - and I'm actually crying as I type, lol - but I'm not sad anymore about the past. and I know if I went through this then some of you did too.

just know we're gonna do this, and we ain't stopping.
we're not crying this time. wwg1wga - TRD
