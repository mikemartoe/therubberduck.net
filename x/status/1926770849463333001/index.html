HOW DO YOU CONNECT Trump, George, a Princess about to be sacrificed, #45, Saudi Arabia and Good Defeats Evil?

location, location, location.

-------

Good Defeats Evil is a bronze sculpture by Soviet/Russian painter and sculptor Zurab Tsereteli. it was gifted to the United Nations in 1990.

it sits directly across the street from Trump World Tower, erected 10 years later.

‼️the statue is a human defeating a dragon, as in the story of Saint George and the Dragon. the tale tells of St. George slaying a dragon that demanded sacrifices from nearby villagers. once the villagers ran out of livestock, they offered humans and when a well-loved princess was selected to be sacrificed, the saint rescues her.‼️

-------

‼️Trump World Tower was built from 1999-2001 [when was "the start"?]. neighbors and politicians in the area opposed the building due to its height and that it tower would dwarf the UN Headquarters building across the street - which it did.‼️😎
