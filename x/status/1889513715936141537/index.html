Timballo di Rigatoni, or baked rigatoni with meatballs.

layers of pasta, basil marinara sauce, fresh mozzarella and parmesan reggiano, and fresh mini meatballs.

the meatballs are key. in a bowl, mix:

1 pound each, beef and pork
3 garlic cloves minced
1.5 cups grated parmesan reggiano
1/3 cup chopped parsley
fresh juice of 1.5 lemons
salt & pepper
1 egg

you could fry them, bake them or put them in an air fryer for 12 to 15 minutes @ 400.

1.) preheat oven to 375. prepare cheeses.

2.) make the meatballs & prepare simple sauce; stew tomatoes peel chop, add basil, garlic, salt then stir over heat - that's it.

3.) cook pasta al dente, 8-10 minutes. drain - then add pasta into the sauce and stir so that everything is coated.

4.) in a soufflé dish rubbed with olive oil; start with a layer of pasta at the bottom, then drop a few meatballs on top of that, then a layer of fresh mozzarella, then a layer of fresh parmesan reggiano.. keep layering until you are out of ingredients - making sure to save enough parmesan and basil for the top.

5.) bake for about 35 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and crispy on top.

bon appétit 🇮🇹
