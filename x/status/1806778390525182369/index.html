🛑 ON THIS DAY IN 1914 🛑

110 years ago today, the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo ignited the spark that started World War I. Despite warnings of potential danger, the Archduke and his wife Sophie visited Bosnia-Herzegovina, a province of Austria-Hungary. A series of assassination attempts followed, culminating in the fatal shooting of both by Gavrilo Princip, a member of a Serbian nationalist group. This event set off a chain reaction of declarations of war among European powers, leading the world into war.

--------------

The license plate of the car in which the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand occurred (sparking World War l) was "A 111 118". This can be read as "Armistice, November 11th, 1918", the day on which that war ended.
