#NeverForget

never forget everything that we’ve learned.

never forget the experience of Q live posting.

never forget the feeling of understanding for the first time what future proves past meant.

never forget all the proofs we did together - and that we kept the receipts.

never forget that nobody walks away from this.

never forget that Q made no promises other than truth - and that it was always our choice to know.

never forget the oath that we all took together.

never forget that we are watching a movie and the news is fake.

never forget the first time DJT spoke directly to us.

never forget the direct access we have to him.

never forget how much time of your life you have invested in this - perhaps a masters, or even a doctorate degree in what we know & how we came to know it.

never forget that we are in this together and that where we go when we go all.
