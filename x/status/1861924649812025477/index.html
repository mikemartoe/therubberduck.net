George W. Bush's younger brother - Marvin P. Bush, owned a company called Securacom [aka, Stratesec] that provided security for the World Trade Center, United Airlines, and Dulles International Airport on September 11, 2001.

** in addition to providing security for the World Trade Center complex up to that day, Stratesec was responsible for installing and operating all video and electronic equipment throughout the premises. **

the company was funded by KuwAm, a Kuwaiti-American investment firm on whose board Marvin Burns also served at the time a favor from the Kuwaitis's for his father's "liberation" of their country.

Marvin was also the Director of the World Trade Center's casualty insurance company - Houston Casualty Insurance - on 9/11/2001. that company terminated its policy on the World Trade Center complex just prior to 9/11.

all facts. all coincidences.
