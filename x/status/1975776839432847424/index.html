‼️BREAKING‼️ at 12:02am President Trump posted the 1963 letter signed by Attorney General Robert F. Kennedy regarding the federalization of the Alabama National Guard under 10 U.S.C. §§ 332–333, in response to Governor Wallace’s obstruction of University of Alabama integration.

That same day (June 11, 1963), Guard troops under federal command escorted Vivian Malone and James Hood onto campus, ending Wallace’s blockade, the “Stand in the Schoolhouse Door”.
