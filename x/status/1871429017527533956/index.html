exceptionally rich hot chocolate that combines the intensity of dark chocolate with sweetness of milk chocolate and fresh cream giving it a silky texture:

Ingredients:
- 2 cups milk
- 2 ounces of 70% cocoa
- 2 ounces 95% cocoa
- 4 ounces milk chocolate
- 1 tablespoon of Dutch-processed cocoa powder
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2/3 cup heavy or whipping cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- heavy pinch of salt

1. **Chop Chocolate:** Finely chop both the dark and milk chocolate. This ensures it melts evenly.

2. **Heat Milk:** In a medium saucepan, warm the milk over medium heat until it begins to steam but not boil.

3. **Combine Ingredients:** Whisk in the cocoa powder, sugar, and salt until they are fully dissolved into the milk.

4. **Melt Chocolate:** Reduce the heat to low, then add the chopped chocolate. Stir continuously until the chocolate has melted completely into the milk.

5. **Add Cream:** Pour in the heavy cream and vanilla extract, stirring to combine.

6. **Simmer:** Allow the mixture to simmer gently for about 2-3 minutes to thicken slightly, stirring occasionally to prevent the chocolate from sticking to the bottom.

7. **Serve:** Pour the hot chocolate into mugs. For an extra touch of luxury, top with whipped cream or a marshmallow, and if you like, a dusting of cocoa powder or chocolate shavings.
