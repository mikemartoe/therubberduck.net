Meet Lucky Larry!!

👉🏼 this is Donald Trump’s nickname for Larry and you’ll find out why now…

————————

On 9/11/2001, #LarrySilverstein just so happened to be absent from work because of a doctor's appointment. He later claimed that the appointment was cancelled, and rather than returning to the top of the tower for his usual breakfast, he decided to walk around New York City. Larry's family was also absent from work that day and escaped harm, including his daughter and son (who was running late).

Lucky Larry was also fortunate enough to have recently taken out a S3.5 Billion insurance policy only weeks before the attacks (July 2001). He went on to attempt to claim that amount twice (2 attacks). Luckily for Larry, he and his family managed to escape death and profit billions from the fall of the towers.
