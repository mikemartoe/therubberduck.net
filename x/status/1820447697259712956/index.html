Level 3 Automation, also known as conditional automation, is a type of advanced driver assistance system (ADAS) that allows your electric car to take over driving responsibilities in certain conditions:

1. The vehicle is on a highway or limited-access road.
2. The car is traveling at a speed below a predetermined limit (e.g., 40 mph).
3. The weather conditions are favorable (e.g., no heavy rain or snow).
4. The road has clear lane markings and is well-maintained.

caveat: the driver is still responsible for the vehicle's operation and must be ready to intervene if the system encounters a situation it cannot handle.
