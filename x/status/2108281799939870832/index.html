💥💥President Trump tells the story of the infamous report his uncle - Dr John G Trump - was commissioned to write about Nicolas Tesla's final works - after Tesla died at the famous New Yorker Hotel.💥💥

‼️HERE'S THE STORY‼️ Nikola Tesla died of heart failure some time between the evening of 5 January and the morning of 8 January when his body was found by a maid in his room at the iconic Hotel New Yorker.

🛑 FACT: during the last years of his life Tesla claimed to have developed advanced technology, including wireless power transmission, a revolutionary torpedo, and a directed-energy weapon - among other things.

👉🏼 during the final months of his life - Tesla's Yugoslavian immigrant nephew, Sava Kosanović, was under surveillance by the FBI as a possible spy.

👉🏼 when Tesla's body was found by the hotel maid on January 8, 1943 it was reported to the FBI that Tesla's nephew, Kosanović and two associates had entered Tesla's room at the Hotel New Yorker and opened his safe after his death looking for his will.

🛑 THIS CAUSED the FBI to immediately open an espionage case concerning Nikola Tesla's research. the FBI brought in the Office of Alien Property Custodian - led by agents Walter Gorsuch and Joseph T. King.

👀 FBI File #: 100-2257 [=17] was created.

‼️THE VERY NEXT DAY‼️ January 9, 1943 - the Office of Alien Property Custodian immediately took possession of the scene and carted away 2 truckloads containing 80 trunks of Tesla's documents and equipment - then brought in MIT Professor and Military Scientist Dr. John G Trump to provide an assessment of whether Tesla's materials could assist the American war effort or become dangerous in enemy hands.

⁉️Why Dr. Trump⁉️

👉🏼 John Trump was brought in specifically because like Tesla, he studied high-voltage electrical engineering under Robert J. Van de Graaff. Trump was already a member of the National Defense Research Committee, chaired by George HW Bush cousin Vannevar Bush. the NDRC coordinated wartime scientific research.

❓❓COINCIDENCE❓❓ it was Vannevar Bush who personally directed Dr. John Trump to study with Van de Graaff many years earlier that would guide Trump's career.

👉🏼 Trump provided his report in a letter that would later be attached to this case called: "Unknown Subjects; Equipment, Experiments and Research of Nikola Tesla, Deceased; Espionage."

👀👀 in this letter, which stated no weapons were found in the work Dr. Trump investigated - he noted, "No investigation was made of material in trunks which had remained untouched in the basement of the New Yorker Hotel for ten years prior to Dr. Tesla's death." 👀👀
