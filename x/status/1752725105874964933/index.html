it took me about 30 years to adopt and own this worldview; that I firmly believe there is enough money floating around in this world for all of us.

someone with a mathematical or financial background would look at my life over the past 30 years and wonder how I lived literally paycheck to paycheck through it all, got married, had a huge wedding, had two kids within the first two years, witnessed and survived 9/11, relocated a huge printing company from NJ to FL over the 10 day Christmas and New Year’s vacation of 2001/02, became a homeowner for nearly 20 years, lost that house during the financial crisis of 2010/11, still with my wife for those same 30 years, and raised 2 gorgeous, smart, and based young men that are still living with me - cuz they want to.

life’s been good to me so far. I find that the more I get along with my life, the easier my life becomes.

I never had a good relationship with money. mom never graduated high school & Dad was the son of a Sicilian immigrant from West Virginia who had the mind and hands of an engineer/carpenter. It just didn’t work out that way.

People say they learned the lessons of their parents by how long & hard they worked and by how much stress they endured, just so that they could retire to a 1 bedroom condo here in florida - with a $1700/month pension - then start living at 65 years old.

that whole mindset was born from the television, and into the books that were used in the schools that we were taught, then onto the movie screens that we watched and now reside in our phones and screens.

fuck all of that.

time for a new global narrative.

I am living the new narrative.
