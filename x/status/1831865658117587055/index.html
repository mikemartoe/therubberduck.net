according to documents filed at Manhattan Surrogate’s Court in 1999, John F. Kennedy Junior's estate at his time of death was up to $100 million.

according to his will, John left his entire estate to his wife, Carolyn Bessett -- and if tragically Carolyn were also to die, which she did, then his estate would go to the children of John’s sister Caroline, her children and RFK Junior.

this included John's TriBeCa apartment and his share of Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis' sprawling Martha's Vineyard estate.

the only personal item mentioned in Junior's will was a scrimshaw set - sailor's carvings made from whales' teeth - that belonged to President Kennedy, who was assassinated 3 days before Junior's third birthday. John left this to his then 6-year-old nephew - John B. Kennedy Schlossberg.

the will doesn't mention the shares John owned in GEORGE Magazine, but specified that all of his money and stocks be left in a trust fund he established in 1983 and revised on Dec. 19, 1997, the same day he wrote the will. the trust was run by Kennedy's cousin, Timothy Shriver.
