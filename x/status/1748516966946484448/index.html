In 2018, a special edition coin was minted to commemorate the 70th anniversary of #Israel's independence. The coin, known as the "#ThirdTempleCoin," featured an image of President Donald Trump alongside #KingCyrus, who 2,500 years ago allowed #Jews to return to #Jerusalem from their exile in #Babylon.

The coin was minted following the announcement that the #AmericanEmbassy would be transferred to Jerusalem in honor of Israel's 70th Independence Day. The coin's design also included a stylized image of the #ThirdTemple, symbolizing the hope for the temple's reconstruction on the Temple Mount.
