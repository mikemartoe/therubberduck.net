👉🏼 if you had no choice but to prosecute a war like this without alerting the masses - to prevent global panic and hysteria - how would you do that?

👉🏼 what if there were no people, television or internet where these battles were fought - could you hide a war?

that might work.

👉🏼 could a planned "border invasion" and global "immigrant crisis" provide a credible narrative and protection for these mass movements?

that might work.

👉🏼 would you tell anyone that you won the war and rescued the survivors before figuring this all out first?

👉🏼 could an "alien narrative" actually be more believable to the masses than what these 'alien subhumans' actually pulled off for millennia?

that just might work.
