READ:

Executive Order 13818
December 20, 2017

Blocking the Property of Persons Involved in Serious Human Rights Abuse or Corruption

---

SUMMARY

on December 20, 2017 - President Trump issued an order stating that if foreign individuals commit severe human rights abuses or corruption, like child trafficking, their assets in the U.S. will be frozen. This means they can't sell, move, or use their property, money, or businesses here.

Additionally, these people are barred from entering the U.S., which stops them from influencing or hiding here. The consequences are severe; their bank accounts are to be seized, business dealings halted and seized, and any support or transactions with them by others would fall under the same consequences of this order.
