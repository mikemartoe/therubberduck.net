Yuri Bezmenov: The Four Stages of Ideological Subversion

"this video will help you understand the #16YearPlan they had for us and our Republic."

---------------

#YuriBezmenov was a former #KGB agent who defected to the West in 1970. In 1984, he gave an interview in which he discussed the Soviet Union's strategy to subvert the West through a process called "#IdeologicalSubversion." According to Bezmenov, this process had four stages:

1.) Demoralization: This stage involves the gradual erosion of a society's moral and ethical values. It takes 15-20 years to complete, as it requires influencing a generation of young people through education and media.

2.) Destabilization: This stage focuses on the economy, foreign relations, and defense systems. It takes 2-5 years to complete and aims to create a crisis in these areas.

3.) Crisis: This stage is a period of chaos and confusion, where society is overwhelmed by the problems created in the previous stage. It takes 6 weeks to complete, and during this time, the public is more likely to accept radical solutions.

4.) Normalization: This stage involves the establishment of a new system, with the government taking control of the economy, media, and other aspects of society. It can last indefinitely, as the new regime consolidates its power and eliminates any opposition.

---------------

Bezmenov's warning was not only about the Soviet Union but also about the dangers of ideological subversion in general. He believed that it was crucial for the West to be aware of these tactics and to resist them.
