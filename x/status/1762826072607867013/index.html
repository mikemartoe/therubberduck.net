I'm a realist with unshakable faith. there is no way they lied to us about everything, except the world being shaped like a ball. I don't even need science or physics. I grew up and was taught just like you were. but I had to let it all go and go with what feels right until new evidence presents itself, this is my truth.

it's funny, once I let go of everything, evidence of truth presented itself immediately in front of me. it filled that void of nothing that they created in all of us, and I haven't been quite the same since - but much better.

I don't know what shape it is, I don't know how big it is. but I believe earth to be fixed and I believe the sun in the moon, whatever they really are, move over us.

i'm OK with this, until God gives me a nudge to take a look at something else.
