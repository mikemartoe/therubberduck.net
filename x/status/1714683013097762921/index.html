Stevie Wonder was never blind.

my father-in-law sang back up for Little Steven Robbins at the Apollo theater in December 1962 where he had a panic attack on stage, dropped the bongos he was playing, bent over to pick them up, and his glasses fell off.
he was never blind.
it was a scam.

coincidentally, his daughter Aisha worked directly for me in 1994/95 at a marketing and publishing company that I ran back then located on route 46 W. in Little Ferry, New Jersey. she answered the phones and got picked up in a limousine every night.
