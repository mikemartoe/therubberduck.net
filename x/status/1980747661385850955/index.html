DID YOU KNOW John F. Kennedy Jr. was not a “Junior” on his third birthday - also the date of his father's funeral.

his father’s full name was John Fitzgerald Kennedy. his own legal name was also John Fitzgerald Kennedy — the same name, which is what made him a true “Jr.” in formal terms - until his father’s assassination, when his name simply became John F. Kennedy.

the media began trolling him by constantly referring to him as “John-John” or “Junior" - knowing he hated both monikers.

he never formally changed his name, but he chose not to use the "Jr." himself.

in adulthood, Kennedy signed his name simply “John Kennedy” or “John F. Kennedy”. socially and professionally (as editor of George magazine, for instance), he preferred not to use “Jr.” because he wanted to be recognized as his own person, not just an extension of his father’s legacy.
