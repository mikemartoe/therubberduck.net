I once heard that the bigger the lie - the more people will believe it on its face.

as it turns out, that was spot-on true.

the thing is, all of the greatest lies of our lifetimes that we believed to be unwaveringly true have become part of the very fabric of who we are; our grandparents, grandparents, etc.

because of this most people truly feel that if they are proving out the generational lies we’ve been told, that [in their hearts] they are proving their ancestors and relatives and trusted loved ones lied to them willfully - and that’s called cognitive dissonance and that’s the hardest barrier to overcome during this awakening process and my opinion is why it’s taking much longer than most people realize anticipated.

imagine having to unlearn everything you’ve been taught or told [like we did 6+ years ago], and replacing it with ——— ???

truth?

who is to say what’s true now?

pick any topic - pick anything at all? is it true? how do you know? how do you really know?

If the only way to believe something as true is to take the word of any size flat blackscreen or book written by man - I’m sorry, I’m not buying it.

unless I could prove it to my heart first.

this is presently where I’m at with everything .
