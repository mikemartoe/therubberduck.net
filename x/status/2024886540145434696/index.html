Keith Allen, director of a 2011 documentary "Unlawful Killing" about the investigation into the deaths of Princess Diana and Dodi Al Fayed, wrote a letter to the public via The Daily Mail on the opening today of the Cannes Film Festival about NOT including the last photo taken of Diana at the accident scene:

👉“This photo was not used in the film for the purpose of shock. It is included as evidence, because it shows clearly that, although Diana had been INJURED in the crash, she was alert and very much alive. I repeat: it is not a picture of a dying woman."
