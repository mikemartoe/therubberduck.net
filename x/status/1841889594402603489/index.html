October 3, 2024, 1:14 PM ET · This post in the monthly archive · Open this post in the X Archive · View this post on X

stop for a second.
think about it logically...
all of these disasters happening over the past eight years - natural or not...
ask yourself - while in the most dangerous life-threatening moments of your life - would you ever take a pause from saving your life and the people around you - just to talk to a mainstream media reporter?

in that same moment, would you think to agree to a private interview over FaceTime and sob and cry on national television?

it doesn't matter whether they manipulate the weather or not.
it doesn't matter whether the events you are witnessing on the television are happening in real life at that moment or not - it doesn't really matter unless you're there.

what matters is what they broadcast to you on the television - the mainstream narrative.

once you understand that it's all narrative, for good or bad - and that the only people on the television are actors - you start to put pieces of the puzzle together.

only then will this shit start making sense, I assure you.
