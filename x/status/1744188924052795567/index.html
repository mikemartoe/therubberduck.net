Katt delves into personal aspects, explaining why he's never been married and addressing the situations involving Taraji P. Henson, #NickCannon, #JonathanMajors, and #KanyeWest. Katt reflects on the comedy landscape of a bygone era, shedding light on the dynamics within the #KingsOfComedy and their negative sentiments toward #BernieMac. This episode is a rollercoaster of humor and unfiltered truths showcasing Katt Williams in a light that goes beyond the stage."
