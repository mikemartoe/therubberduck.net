Epstein’s arrest in July 2019 was led by the Southern District of New York… | The X Archive | The Rubber Duck™

July 10, 2025, 10:07 PM ET · This post in the monthly archive · Open this post in the X Archive · View this post on X

Epstein’s arrest in July 2019 was led by the Southern District of New York (SDNY) in partnership with:

•FBI
•DOJ
•NYPD

NYPD personnel embedded in federal task forces participated in the investigation as part of broader operations centered in New York.

The FBI and NYPD work together on several joint task forces, particularly those involving:

•Child exploitation
•Sex trafficking
•Human trafficking
•Terrorism

In the Epstein case, the FBI’s Child Exploitation and Human Trafficking Task Force, based in New York, included NYPD detectives embedded in the unit.
