‼️CITIZEN'S ARREST‼️

interesting point of fact - through a combination of statutes and common law, and the varying wording the following rule:

"When a felony has in fact occurred, and a private citizen has reasonable/probable grounds to believe the person committed it, a citizen's arrest can be made until law enforcement arrives.”

👉🏼 is a well-established common-law rule that still exists in a number the following jurisdictions:

Alaska
California
Florida (through common law rather than an explicit citizen’s arrest statute)
Idaho
Iowa
Massachusetts
Michigan
Mississippi
Missouri
Nevada
New Jersey
New Mexico
New York
North Carolina
Oregon
Pennsylvania
South Carolina
Utah
Virginia
Washington
Wisconsin

👉🏼 in many jurisdictions, if a citizen’s arrest is lawful, only the amount of force reasonably necessary to detain the person is permitted. ⚠️ excessive force can create criminal and civil liability, even if the arrest itself was lawful.

**side-note: all of the states listed above allow for either open or concealed carry or both.
