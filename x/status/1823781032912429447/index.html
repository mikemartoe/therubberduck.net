imagine being evacuated from the 26th floor of 1 Liberty Plaza in Manhattan on… | The X Archive | The Rubber Duck™

August 14, 2024, 1:57 PM ET · This post in the monthly archive · Open this post in the X Archive · View this post on X

imagine being evacuated from the 26th floor of 1 Liberty Plaza in Manhattan on the morning of September 11 after hearing an explosion from what you later learned came from the north tower of the world trade center, you were literally told to head west towards the Hudson river - then as you were standing at the river waiting for a nearby fisherman or ferry boat to come collect you and whisk you over to Jersey City or Hoboken - you literally watched the south side of Tower 2 explode, then after making it to the other side you crawl onto one of the who knows how many buses were waiting to take us all home one by one like a bunch of zombies - only to walk into your house and find that somehow not only did everyone in your house see a plane - but anyone who wasn't there physically saw a plane too - but when both you and your dad [who you were with that day] stand there and call them crazy to their face because you were there - their mind melts and they fall into depression and doubt because they believe we are mentally ill and scarred for life. fortunately for us, the advent of severe PTSD meds were readily available from the earlier war Iraq - so we gobbled them up with the newly discovered alcohol of choice we had due to alcoholism or drug addictions we picked up along the way to cope, being that we were literally fucking insane. BUT the prescribing docs all promised that the debilitating 24/7 fear, panic and anxiety would disappear... and sure enough, it did. the combination of both zoloft and whiskey in fact cured me - it wasn't that I stopped believing there was no plane, it's that I didn't have the energy to care, or the energy to do anything really except eat and drink as much alcohol and take as much xanax as possible so that I could sleep as much as possible. severe PTSD meds make your goal in life to sleep so you don't feel the pain. the meds took away my dreams, so I would literally looked forward to the blackness every night. on September 10, 2001 I was 220 pounds, good-looking and athletically built as I had been my whole life up to that point. I was a proud God-fearing father of two boys in diapers with nothing but success and the joy of life in front of me. I was happy, popular, had hundreds of friends and at least 10 close friends that were considered family - living in a beautiful home in the suburbs of New York City, nestled in Bergen county New Jersey. On September 11, 2014 - I was a 415 pound alcoholic addicted to xanax who had missed about 10 of my sons' child rearing years with a marriage teetering on divorce...

that version of me ended on that day and here I am now.

but I'm just one dude, think about what happened to everyone else, worse or better but still with a broken mind - all because of this enemy we are fighting today.

what a fuckshow.
