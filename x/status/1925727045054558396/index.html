‼️WOW‼️ in a recent experiment conducted by the good humanitarians over at CERN the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) transformed lead into gold by stripping protons from lead nuclei (82 protons) to create gold nuclei (79 protons), a process confirmed by the ALICE detector team earlier this month. it produced 29 trillionths of a gram of gold that decayed within a microsecond.

this is alchemy, specifically chrysopoeia, the ancient pursuit of turning base metals into gold.
