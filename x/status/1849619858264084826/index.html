in the United States alone, an estimated 460,000 children are reported missing every year - per the Federal Bureau of Investigation and NCIC: 👉🏼 https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/2015-ncic-missing-person-and-unidentified-person-statistics.pdf/view

the average sports stadium holds 60,000+ fans.

that means every year in the United States alone 8 sports stadiums filled with underage children simply disappear.

HOW IS THAT EVEN REAL?

here are how other countries that track the number of missing children annually measure up to the USA:

Australia: 20,000
Canada: 45,288
Germany: 100,000
India: 96,000
Jamaica: 1,984 [2015]
Russia: 45,000 [2015]
Spain: 20,000
United Kingdom: 112,853
United States: 460,000

in many countries, statistics on missing children are not even available; and, unfortunately, even available statistics may be inaccurate due to: under-reporting/under-recognition; inflation; incorrect database entry of case information; and deletion of records once a case is closed.
👉🏼 https://globalmissingkids.org/awareness/missing-children-statistics/
