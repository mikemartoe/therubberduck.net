... ON HOW CHEMTRAILS DESTROY HUMANITY & EARTH...

when poisonous heavy metals like aluminum, barium, strontium, cadmium and lead are sprayed and fall to the earth, they accumulate in the soil - and stay there.

⸻

Here’s what happens when toxic metals enter the soil:

🧲 They bind to soil particles
•Especially in clay and organic-rich soils, metals like aluminum and barium bind tightly.
•Over time, this changes soil pH, structure, and microbial life.

🌱 They are absorbed by plants
•Plants absorb certain metals, especially strontium (similar to calcium) and aluminum (especially in acidic soils).
•This pathway into the food chain.

💧 They leach into water
•Metals leach into groundwater or run off into surface water, especially in sandy or acidic soils.

🧬 They disrupt soil biology
•Soil microbes and fungi are highly sensitive to toxic metals.
•This reduces decomposition, nitrogen cycling, and root symbiosis.

⸻

Permanence in Soil:
•Heavy metals don’t break down—they’re elements.
•Once in the soil, they persist indefinitely unless actively removed.
•They migrate slowly over time, especially if the soil becomes more acidic or waterlogged.
