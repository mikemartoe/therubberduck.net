here's a list of significant federal laws and executive orders from January 2017 to January 2021.

if you don't know this - you should:

--------------

Executive Orders (EOs):

EO 13766 - Expediting Environmental Reviews and Approvals for High Priority Infrastructure Projects (2017)
EO 13767 - Border Security and Immigration Enforcement Improvements (2017)
EO 13769 - Protecting the Nation from Foreign Terrorist Entry into the United States (2017, later revised)
EO 13771 - Reducing Regulation and Controlling Regulatory Costs (2017)
EO 13772 - Core Principles for Regulating the United States Financial System (2017)
EO 13780 - Protecting the Nation from Foreign Terrorist Entry into the United States (2017, revised version of EO 13769)
EO 13795 - Implementing an America-First Offshore Energy Strategy (2017)
EO 13806 - Addressing Trade Agreement Violations and Abuses (2017)
EO 13817 - Comprehensive Plan for Reorganizing the Executive Branch (2017)
EO 13825 - Strengthening Retirement Security in America (2018)
EO 13839 - Securing the Information and Communications Technology and Services Supply Chain (2018)
EO 13848 - Imposing Certain Sanctions in the Event of Foreign Interference in a United States Election (2018)
EO 13859 - Strengthening Retirement Savings for Americans Through Enhanced Fiduciary Rules (2019)
EO 13902 - Executive Order on Modernizing and Reforming the Assessment and Hiring of Federal Job Candidates (2020)
EO 13917 - America's Foreign Policy (2020)
EO 13920 - Executive Order on the Administration of the Foreign Agents Registration Act of 1938, as Amended (2020)
EO 13925 - Creating Schedule F in the Excepted Service (2020, aimed at federal employee unions but was reversed by Biden in 2021)

--------------

Major Federal Laws:

Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (2017): Sweeping tax reform, lowering corporate and personal income tax rates.
American Health Care Act (AHCA): While it passed the House, it did not become law due to Senate changes and lack of support.
Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2018: Included numerous provisions on spending, policy riders, and disaster relief.
Right to Try Act (2017): Allows terminally ill patients to access certain investigational drugs.
Music Modernization Act (2018): Updates music licensing and copyright laws for the digital age.
First Step Act (2018): Criminal justice reform focusing on sentencing laws and rehabilitation.
CARES Act (Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act) (2020): Provided economic relief in response to the COVID-19 pandemic.
Families First Coronavirus Response Act (2020): Mandated paid leave and expanded unemployment benefits related to the COVID-19 outbreak.
Defense Spending Bill (2021): Annual defense authorization act, which often includes policy provisions beyond defense.
