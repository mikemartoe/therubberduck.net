DJT posted this image with Macaulay Culkin attending the "Fountain of Peace" Tree Lighting Ceremony at the Plaza Hotel on December 5, 1991.

👉🏼 this was shortly before the release of Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, where Trump made a cameo appearance in exchange for allowing filming at the hotel.

👉🏼 Director Chris Columbus, said that during early screenings - audiences cheered when Trump appeared on screen - so he took the deal, shot it in Trump Tower and left Trump in the movie to deliver his famous line to Macaulay.
