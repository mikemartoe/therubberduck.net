🛑 DID YOU KNOW 🛑

#1] #USMarshals have a wide range of powers throughout the nation, including:

a. Apprehending fugitives
b. Operating the federal witness protection program
c. Managing and selling seized assets
d. Transporting federal prisoners
e. Providing security for federal courthouses and judges
f. Enforcing court orders and warrants
g. Assisting in the apprehension of federal escapees

#2] U.S. Marshals do have the authority to deputize, which is called deputation. This process allows the U.S. Marshals to temporarily grant their law enforcement powers to other individuals, typically to assist with specific tasks or operations.

#3] U.S. Marshals have the authority to cross jurisdictions, as they are a federal law enforcement agency. This means they can operate across state lines and in any jurisdiction within the United States. This allows them to carry out their duties, such as apprehending fugitives and transporting prisoners, regardless of the location.
