The word "tesseract" has its origins in Greek. It is a combination of "tetra," meaning four, and "hypercube." A tesseract is a four-dimensional geometric shape, essentially a cube extended into the fourth dimension. The term was coined in 1888 by Charles Howard Hinton, a mathematician and science fiction writer, as part of his exploration of higher-dimensional geometry.
