section 10.3 -- #LawOfWar Manual:

10.3 PROTECTED PERSON STATUS

"In general, the GC uses the concept of protected person to define the individuals who are entitled to receive its protections. Principally, protected persons include persons of enemy nationality living in the territory of a belligerent State and the inhabitants of occupied territories. Even if persons are not protected persons under the GC, other rules may be applicable to them. For example, certain baseline rules apply to the treatment of all detainees, including those who are not protected persons or POWs."
