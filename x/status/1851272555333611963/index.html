understanding "DELTA's" in the Q drops.

a delta - simply put - is the anniversary date of a past Q drop. for example - the below Q drop below was first posted on 10/29/2024, 7 years ago today, so it's a 7 year delta.

**ASLO: the term "delta" was used for #Qproofs to mark instances when the Q team would post a drop, and within 60 seconds, Trump would tweet a confirmation of that drop using our comms. this was part of the ongoing "authentication process" Q and Anons used to constantly confirm & maintain the operation's integrity and credibility.

"why is it relevant today? what do you do with delta's?"

• for me - delta's confirm "the plan" - in terms of what has happened, and what is to come that the TV and internet have not reported on publicly yet.

• delta's remind us of where we were at a certain moment in time - which has been nearly impossible to do during this 8 year information war.

• look at this Q drop posted 7 years ago today. some of the information in this drop were in reference to events that were happening at that time. some of it made sense to us at the time - and, as you can plainly see, some of it really hits home now.
