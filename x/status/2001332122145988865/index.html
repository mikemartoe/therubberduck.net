‼️The COVFEFE Act of 2017‼️

👉🏼 or The Communications Over Various Feeds Electronically for Engagement Act of 2017

👉🏼 the COVFEFE Act proposed amending the Presidential Records Act (PRA) to:
🎯explicitly define social media posts (e.g., tweets) as official presidential records
🎯require that electronic communications by the POTUS be preserved, archived and transferred to the National Archives (NARA) as part of the presidential record.

THIS WAS AN EARLY WINK - as the covfefe act went to committee and later failed BUT got lots immediate attention for a variety of reasons.

**NOTE: courts later ruled that Trump’s social media posts were official records and could not be blocked or deleted arbitrarily.

double meanings exist.
