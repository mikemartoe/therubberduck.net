Solomon’s Temple (First Temple) was destroyed by the Babylonians under… | The X Archive | The Rubber Duck™

March 6, 2026, 7:42 AM ET · This post in the monthly archive · Open this post in the X Archive · View this post on X

Solomon’s Temple (First Temple) was destroyed by the Babylonians under Nebuchadnezzar II in 586 BC during the siege of Jerusalem.

The Second Temple was completed around 516 BC after the return of Jewish exiles from Babylon (during the Persian Empire).

The Second Temple was destroyed by the Romans in 70 AD during the First Jewish–Roman War under Roman general Titus.

The Dome of the Rock, built in 691 AD by the Umayyad Caliphate, sits on the Temple Mount, Judaism’s holiest site and Islam’s third-holiest site (after Mecca and Medina).
