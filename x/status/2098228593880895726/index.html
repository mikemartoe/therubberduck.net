‼️NO PLANE HIT THE SECOND TOWER ON 9/11‼️

ask any graphic designer, digital video editor or special effects tech [at the time] - and every one of them will tell you that it would be nearly impossible to remove a plane from these clips.

HOWEVER

👉🏼 adding one via layering/masking = 17 minutes or less [in 2001].

which is exactly how much time they had from when flight 11 crashed into the North Tower [8:46 a.m.] and 9:03 a.m. - when "flight 175 crashed into the South Tower", allegedly.

👉🏼 there was no live feed of tower 2 exploding "from the plane crash" either. that was an illusion. the live feeds were fixed on Tower 1 because that's where the alleged first plane crash was.

👉🏼 only the fireball from the explosion of Tower 2 could be seen in the lower left-hand corner of all the live feeds at that moment in time - no impact was ever captured live as many thought and felt - the livestream fixed on the Tower 1 immediately panned down and to the exploding fireball. no plane.

BUT HOW?

you'll appreciate this when you look back at the original broadcast footage yourself.

THERE WAS 17 minutes in between both explosions - together with a delayed livestream [no impact footage appeared anywhere until well after the event]. this was more than enough time, distraction and cover for an average graphic designer with a steady hand to simply create a digital plane and layer it onto a few second clip of impact footage they already had from another source or location - which is why only a select few impact clips are authorized or considered "authentic" by mainstream media.

👉🏼 all of the "authentic" or "approved" impact clips from Tower 2 display obvious evidence of masking; one featuring the nose of the plane popping through the east side of Tower 2 and another clip features the plane crashing into the west side of Tower 2 but the plane's wing disappears as it passes a church just before impact - obviously the graphic artist did not cut out enough mask to account for the wing.

this could've only happened if they were layering & masking. it was a common mistake at the time. still is for me as I still use old software. takes a few minutes to fix, but they didn't have that - it was worth the risk, because it was part of their plan and everyone was in on it.

👉🏼 they only needed enough time to trick you into believing you saw planes impacting buildings live on the television.

truth is - the only people that actually saw what happened were the people there that day to see it and are still alive, and I'm one of them. sadly - there were a lot of LARPS there that day too, and sadly - many of them are still alive too, and on TV.

that's been my story - and explanation for what I did not see that day - and I'm sticking to it.
