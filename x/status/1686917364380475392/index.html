#AnonIsland

On Anon Island, where the oceans kiss the shore,
A gathering of truth tellers, Anons, evermore,
Patriotic souls, from distant lands they roam,
In search of God's wisdom, they find their home.

They talk of love and grace, in earnest embrace,
With plans to heal the world, a better place,
Cures for every ailment, hope blooms anew,
Children revered and cherished, their dreams come true.

From every corner of the world, they come,
United in purpose, their work is never done,
In the shadows of the night, they scheme and dream,
For a future where all can thrive, and be free.

Anon Island, where hearts are intertwined,
In the light of truth, their brilliance shines,
A sanctuary of love and grace,
A self-sustainable haven, led by Autists' embrace.

Children are protected, safe and sound,
Their laughter echoes through the island, happiness bound,
Anons' minds, like stars, guiding the way,
Towards a brighter world, where love will stay.

Their brilliance, like a beacon, guides the lost,
In the realm of truth, no line is crossed,
With truth as their compass, they'll forge ahead,
On Anon Island, where truth's path is tread.

In this haven of Anons, love & truth abounds,
In every whisper and in every sound,
A legacy of compassion they'll impart,
For on Anon Island, God's love reigns in every heart.
