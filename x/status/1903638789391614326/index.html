‼️James Jesus Angleton‼️ graduated from Yale ☠️ in 1941, he joined the OSS (Office of Strategic Services) during World War II, where he ran counterintelligence operations in Europe, mastering propaganda and disinformation techniques. After the war, he continued working in intelligence as the OSS transitioned into the CIA.

Angleton's was obsessed with counterintelligence. In 1947, he joined the CIA as a senior aide, focusing on uncovering Soviet espionage, particularly after the Soviets detonated their first atomic bomb in 1949, which Angleton believed was aided by spies. He rose through the ranks and, in 1954, was appointed Deputy Director and Chief of Counterintelligence under CIA Director Allen Dulles.

Angleton was deeply involved in protecting the CIA's most guarded secrets, including UFO-related intelligence and the controversial MJ-12 program, which dealt with extraterrestrial life and technology. This secrecy was authorized by a directive from President Truman in 1947, limiting access to a select few. Angleton’s aggressive counterintelligence efforts of wiretapping and bypassing legal channels under Truman's protection gave him unprecedented and dangerous access to anyone for any reason.

In 1963 President Kennedy, who sought UFO information from the CIA, requested a review of classified UFO files to share with NASA and the Soviets. Angleton and Dulles resisted, viewing this as a threat to national security.

👀 Kennedy issued a memorandum to Angleton on this matter a few days before his assassination.👀

During Angleton’s tenure it was alleged publicly that the ClA was directly involved in Marilyn Monroe’s 1962 death, due to her knowledge of Kennedy’s UFO interests.

Angleton retired in 1974 and died in 1987.
