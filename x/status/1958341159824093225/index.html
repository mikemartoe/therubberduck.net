Rosemary's Baby was released on June 12, 1968 and directed by Roman Polanski, a convicted pedophile.

more about Polanski in a minute...

THE MOVIE is "about a young couple - Rosemary and her husband Guy - move into a New York apartment with a dark past. Rosemary becomes pregnant and suffers disturbing dreams and illness. She suspects that their neighbors and her husband Guy are part of a satanic cult.
In the end, Rosemary learns her baby is the son of satan."

BACK TO POLANSKI... a year after Rosemary's Baby was released in theaters, Polanski's wife, Sharon Tate - who was 8 1/2 months pregnant was found brutally murdered, along with their unborn baby and 4 others in Los Angeles during what was called a satanic ritual. members of the Manson Family cult broke into their home that night and were blamed and charged for the murders.
