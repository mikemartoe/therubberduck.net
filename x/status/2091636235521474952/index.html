‼️YOU'LL FIND OUT‼️

ON THE EVENING OF October 5, 2017 President Donald Trump suddenly - and without warning - summoned the White House Press Corps without warning to document a dinner he was holding with the military leaders.

the press were rushed into the grand State Dining Room, where they walked into a scene of the President, his highest-ranking military aides and their wives posing for a group photo.

👉🏼 Trump gestured to the reporters in the room and famously states, "you guys know what this represents? maybe it's the calm before the storm - could be the calm, the calm before the storm."

👉🏼 a reporter asks: "what storm Mr. President?"

👉🏼 POTUS replied: "You'll find out."

he was right - we have been.
