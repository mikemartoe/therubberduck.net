NO JOKE - if you know me, you know I don't dream, let alone remember whether or not I ever do.

"HELLO GEORGE! let me open with BIG order of 2020 election please - family size, i'll share it with the table. i'm not a big fan of intermezzo, but if you have any upcoming Q drops, I'll have a couple of those - if not no big deal, they're just for me anyway nobody else at the table likes them [wink], hahaha... anyway - I've heard all about this famous dish of yours - everybody talks about it, your "crimes against children". I heard you make it with a special sauce that takes 10 years to ferment! that true? wow, let me have a full order and give me plenty of time to digest it. also - bring the kids some crayon and paper please, along with a "share plate" if that's OK. bring them each half an order of epstein clients and a half order of alien disclosure please. I know you guys have the best cannolis and spumoni - but I'm gonna go with 10 days of darnkess and tribunals livestreamed. I know I know. it's not everyone's favorite, but I couldn't think of a better way to finish off this spectacular meal. THANKS GEORGE!!"

is that crazy or what?
