you don't see or think about what we see or think about yet because you are… | The X Archive | The Rubber Duck™

March 24, 2025, 8:45 AM ET · with photo · This post in the monthly archive · Open this post in the X Archive · View this post on X

you don't see or think about what we see or think about yet because you are still hardwired to a mental and emotional control system that we broke free from. we will never again see things the same way, your way. the fear that you are feeling is brought about by your reptilian brain that has served as first and only filter for every thought and decision you have made over the course your life.

when you realize that you consciously control that filter, you begin to widen the aperture a bit,
and you start to question things - and when you can't find the answers to your own questions, natural willful curiosity will lead you directly to those answers where you will be presented with a choice, the choice to know or not - with the understanding that the result of that choice may change your view on certain things, for good or bad.

unfortunately, many people make the choice to know but they fail to take the next steps - exercising critical thinking and discernment skills - which leads to understanding and balance. it doesn't make you right or wrong, it just helps you to absorb & understand. you cannot fear something that you understand and you can never be forced into group-think mentality when you have a firm understanding of the subject matter.

I hope this helps ❤️
