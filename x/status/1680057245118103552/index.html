The #PinealGland is the size of a grain of rice, this tiny gland plays an essential role in how we sleep, perform, make decisions, and perceive reality.

It acts as the body’s light meter. It receives light information from the eyes and then sends out hormonal messages to the body, known as the #CircadianRhythm.

A healthy-functioning pineal is essential for psychological development, peak performance, and spiritual awakening.

In the 1990s, British scientist #JenniferLuke discovered high concentrations of #fluoride in the pineal gland of her subjects.

Fluoride, commonly found in most municipal water as well as #pesticides, accumulates in the pineal more than any other part of the body.

This accumulation of fluoride forms #PhosphateCrystals, creating a hard shell around the pineal called #calcification.

Studies show that pineal gland calcification:

• Lowers productions of #melatonin
• Impairs the sleep-wake cycle
• Disrupts the regulation of the circadian rhythm

Fluoridated water is the chief cause of calcification in the pineal gland.

** Evidence suggests that children reach puberty earlier now as a consequence of fluoridated water.

** The same studies show fluoride water exposure also decreases melatonin and accelerates sexual development in females.
