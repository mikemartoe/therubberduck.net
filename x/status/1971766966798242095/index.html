“The Extraordinary Equation of George Van Tassel” (aired June 18, 1964)

----------

George Van Tassel was a California-based ufologist / a “contactee” who had close encounters and communications with extraterrestrials.

his work [until 1978] focused on the construction of a device called the Integratron, a dome-shaped structure that embodied rejuvenation, time-travel, and anti-gravity effects.

through Van Tassel's alien encounters he learned a formula for time travel linked to Earth’s magnetic field, as he believed and was able to prove that earth's geomagnetism plays a role in how time and frequency interact.

from this experience he developed the equation: F = 1 / T, where F is frequency and T is time and was able to prove that conventional electronics work in two dimensions (electric and magnetic), but that there is a third zone — a “time zone” — which "records” past events - proving it is possible to “play back” historical events by accessing earth's magnetic fields.

Van Tassel was convinced at the time of this interview that this formula was already in place at that time.

the structure he constructed is built without metal fasteners (to avoid magnetic interference), and is intended to concentrate earth’s energy - through “sacred geometry,” magnetic resonance, etc. - to achieve rejuvenation, anti-aging, and time effects — a device capable of “neutralizing time’s effect” on living beings — effectively to heal or “recharge” cells.
