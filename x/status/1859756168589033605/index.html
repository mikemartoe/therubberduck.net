Mike Pence's Exit and SAM239

--------------

on July 2, 2019 - two days before the Celebration of America event at the National Mall, on July 4th, Mike Pence flew to NH allegedly to meet with the Kissinger Group. he left in a private Gulfstream N996GA bound for Indiana, flying under the Vice Presidential call sign of AF2.

whatever plane the vice president flies in - the call sign for that aircraft becomes AF2.

half way to Indiana, the plane was ordered by the White House to turn and head back to DC.

when this happened - the plane's call sign was changed to SAM239 - or - Special Air Mission 239.
