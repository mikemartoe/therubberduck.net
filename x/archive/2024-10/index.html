The Uniform Code of Military Justice (UCMJ) is a set of federal laws that govern the members of the United States Armed Forces. Here's an overview:

Purpose: It provides for the maintenance of discipline, uniformity, and efficiency within the military branches. It applies to active duty members, reservists, and in certain circumstances, even retired military personnel and civilians serving with or accompanying the armed forces.

Structure: The UCMJ outlines offenses, procedures for courts-martial, and rights of the accused. Offenses range from minor infractions like missing a formation to severe crimes such as murder or treason.
