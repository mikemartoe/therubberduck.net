National Park Service, “Battleground National Cemetery: Gettysburg Address Plaque,” inscription · nps.gov

“The Bliss copy is the only version to which Lincoln affixed his signature, and because of the care in its preparation (including a title, the signature and date) it has become the standard version of the address and the source for most facsimile reproductions of Lincoln’s Gettysburg Address.”

National Park Service, Lincoln Memorial, “Gettysburg Address” · nps.gov
