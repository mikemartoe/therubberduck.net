About David & AORHELP

David Lester Straight

David Straight was an Oregon rancher and self-taught legal and genealogy researcher. By his own account in the Out of Babylon seminar, he served in the U.S. Navy, helped families with court cases since 1989, and studied genealogy for over 50 years.

  • Born April 20, 1963; passed away April 1, 2025.
  • Recorded the eight-part Out of Babylon series June 4–6, 2021 at Church of Glad Tidings.
  • Focused his seminars on state-national status, common law, equity, trust law, contract law, and the history of corporate vs. constitutional governance.

Sources: Video Room seminar archive (l3696eadb94–l3d167ed6ea); White Funeral Home & Freedom Radio memorial notices.

aorhelp.com & the Freedom Bundle

David’s companion site, aorhelp.com, was dedicated to helping people prepare and record an Affidavit of Repudiation (“Become an American state National as taught by David Straight”) and claim their minor estate.

  • Steps to Freedom: God & natural law = equity; Land = common law; Air = trust law; Water = contract law.
  • Freedom Bundle items: Recording Cover Sheet, Affidavit of Repudiation, Certified Proof of Service, Judgment of Un-Rebutted Affidavit, Deed of Re-Conveyance, Declaration of Status, plus passport application instructions.
