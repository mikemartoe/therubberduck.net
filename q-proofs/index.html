Q Proofs

A Q proof is a Q drop confirmed publicly by Donald Trump. Drops signed Q+ are attributed to President Trump himself. A delta proof is a drop that proves true on its anniversary date; see Deltas.

Drop
A post by the Q team.
Delta
A drop’s anniversary. Each day, anons read the drops posted on the same date in past years, 2017 to 2022, to see how they read today.
Delta proof
A delta becomes a proof when the past drop proves true on its anniversary date.
Q proof
A Q drop confirmed publicly by Donald Trump. Drops signed Q+ are attributed to President Trump.
